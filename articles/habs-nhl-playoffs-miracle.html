Montreal Canadiens Complete Game 7 OT Miracle to Reach Eastern Conference Finals

The Montreal Canadiens have punched their ticket to the Eastern Conference Finals in heart-stopping fashion, defeating the Buffalo Sabres 4-3 in overtime of Game 7 thanks to a breathtaking goal from forward Alex Newhook.

1. A Game 7 For The Ages

Trailing 3-1 halfway through the third period, Montreal's playoff campaign appeared to be drawing to a close. But a powerplay laser from Cole Caufield followed by a scrambling equalizer from Nick Suzuki with 1:42 remaining forced sudden-death overtime, sending Bell Centre into pandemonium.

2. Newhook's Golden Moment

Just 4 minutes into the extra session, Newhook intercepted a clearing pass along the boards, drove hard to the net, and tucked a backhand past Sabres goaltender Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen.

"This team has zero quit. When we were down two in the third, nobody panicked. We trusted our system, played for each other, and executed." — Alex Newhook

3. What This Means for Montreal

This marks the Habs' first Eastern Conference Finals appearance since their storied 2021 Cup run. The young core has matured under pressure, demonstrating resiliency and high-octane transitional speed.
