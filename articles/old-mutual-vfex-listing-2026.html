Old Mutual Returns to Trading on VFEX After Historic 6-Year Suspension

In a milestone moment for Zimbabwe's financial sector, insurance and asset management giant Old Mutual officially resumed trading on the local capital market, listing its shares on the US dollar-denominated Victoria Falls Stock Exchange (VFEX) after a six-year hiatus.

1. The End of a 6-Year Freeze

Trading in Old Mutual shares was originally suspended in June 2020 amid exchange rate volatility and policy interventions surrounding dual-listed equities. The resolution of regulatory discussions and transition to the offshore-focused VFEX represents a major breakthrough for institutional investors.

2. Boost for Market Liquidity

Market analysts expect the VFEX listing to boost liquidity and attract foreign direct investment. With annual inflation stabilizing at a 30-year low of 4.2% for the first seven months of 2026, institutional sentiment has improved significantly.

"The return of Old Mutual to active trading is a vote of confidence in our dollarized exchange infrastructure and economic stabilization efforts." — Financial Sector Analyst

3. What This Means for Shareholders

Local and offshore shareholders who held frozen Old Mutual assets can now actively trade shares in US dollars, restoring balance sheet valuations and reopening dividend distribution channels.
