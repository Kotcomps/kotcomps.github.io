Rockford 'Roki' Josphat Sparks Viral Storm Following Lodge Drama Video

Zimbabwean Urban Grooves pioneer Rockford "Roki" Josphat has found himself at the center of intense social media debate after a viral video recorded at a Harare lodge surfaced online, showing a physical confrontation over an alleged unpaid bill.

1. The Viral Incident

In the leaked video footage, an unidentified woman is seen grabbing the hitmaker by his dreadlocks while demanding payment. Roki can be heard defending himself in the clip, exclaiming: "They drank my beer. They drank my Hennessy!"

2. Social Media Division

The clip quickly went viral across TikTok, Facebook, and WhatsApp groups, generating thousands of memes and reactions. While some fans defended the artist citing privacy concerns and lack of context, others criticized the public disturbance.

"They drank my beer, they drank my Hennessy..." — Roki Josphat in the viral video

3. Status & Follow Up

Roki has not yet released an official statement addressing the video, but industry peers have urged fans to avoid rushing to judgment until official facts are clarified.
