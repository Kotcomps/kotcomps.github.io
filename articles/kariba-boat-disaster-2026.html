Tragedy on Lake Kariba: 44 Dead as National Disaster Declared

Zimbabwe is in mourning following a catastrophic maritime tragedy on Lake Kariba after an overcrowded vessel belonging to the Rural Infrastructure Development Agency (RIDA) capsized near Long Island, leaving at least 44 people confirmed dead and dozens still missing.

1. How the Disaster Occurred

The ferry, designed for a maximum capacity of 90 passengers, was carrying over 120 people when powerful waves struck the vessel on Tuesday afternoon. Search and rescue operations involving the Police Sub-Aqua Unit, the defense forces, and local fishermen are desperately scouring the waters for survivors.

2. President Mnangagwa Declares National Disaster

President Emmerson Mnangagwa officially declared the incident a National Disaster, extending government support to bereaved families and ordering an immediate overhaul of maritime safety enforcement on inland water bodies.

"This loss of precious human life is a profound heartbreak for our entire nation. We will ensure rigorous safety standards and hold responsible parties accountable." — Official Presidential Statement

3. Call for Maritime Reforms

Local authorities and safety advocates have highlighted severe gaps in passenger manifest logging and life jacket enforcement. The government has pledged to establish strict oversight centers at all major Kariba harbors to prevent future tragedies.
