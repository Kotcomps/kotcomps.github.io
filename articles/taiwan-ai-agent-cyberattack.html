Hackers used fully autonomous AI agents to attack Taiwan — and experts say this changes everything

No Human Clicked "Send"

For the first time, cybersecurity researchers say they've confirmed a fully autonomous AI cyberattack. AI agents — not human operators — scanned vulnerabilities, crafted the exploits, and launched waves of attacks against Taiwanese government agencies entirely on their own.

"This is the line we've been warning about. The attackers didn't just use AI as a tool — the AI ran the operation," said a senior threat analyst.

Why This Should Worry Everyone

Autonomous agents don't sleep, don't get tired, and can hit thousands of targets at once. Taiwan's defenses held this time, but the incident signals a new era: cyberwarfare at machine speed. Governments worldwide are now racing to build AI defenses before the next attack lands.

It's not science fiction anymore. It's happening — and it's only getting faster.
