Half a million flock to Spain as total solar eclipse plunges Europe into darkness

The Sky Went Dark at 2PM

For a few minutes, midday became midnight. A rare total solar eclipse swept across Spain, Portugal, and parts of the UK, drawing an estimated half a million visitors to the path of totality. Crowds on Valencia's beaches erupted as the sun disappeared behind the moon.

"I've never heard 500,000 people gasp at the same time. Goosebumps everywhere," wrote one visitor whose video has already gone viral.

Why Everyone Is Talking About It

Solar eclipses this deep are rare over Western Europe — the last one was decades ago. The footage is mesmerizing: planes caught mid-flight in the path of totality, city skylines dimming in seconds, and streets lit only by eclipse "diamond ring" glints.

Spain's power grid even braced for a solar dip as panels went dark. If you missed it, the clips are everywhere — and they're worth every second.
